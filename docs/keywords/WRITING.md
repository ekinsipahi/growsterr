# Writing the pSEO pages: brief for writers

Every page in `page-plan.csv` gets written from this brief. The engine, the checks and the
format are in `docs/CONTENT.md` (Turkish); read it fully. This file adds what the keyword
research asks for.

## What Growsterr sells (never write anything that contradicts this)

- **One product.** A monthly growth package, paid as a Stripe subscription. Three packages:
  {starter_name}, {rising_name}, {pro_name}. Prices and typical monthly ranges come only from
  placeholders: `{starter_price}`, `{starter_audience}`, `{from_price}`… Never type a price or a
  range by hand.
- **How it works.**
  - We show the creator's profile to real people through promoted campaigns in our partner
    network, every day.
  - The people who like what they see follow. Many also like, watch and comment.
  - The customer tells us the city, region or country the audience should come from.
  - The dashboard shows what our delivery system measured: profile visits and new followers,
    day by day.
- **Never.**
  - Promising numbers or timing on one specific post, video, Reel or live stream. What is
    true:
    - On {rising_name} and {pro_name}, the customer chooses which posts or videos get the
      attention (a package feature).
    - On YouTube, the customer can name one video (at checkout or later in their account): new
      viewers are sent to it, subscribe first, then watch.

    Say those, but never promise likes, views, comments, watch time or a moment on that post.
  - Describing the campaign's technical method beyond "promoted campaigns in our partner
    network". Never say we do or don't buy ads on a platform. "We don't run ads from your
    account" is fine: we never touch the customer's account.
  - Bots, fake accounts, instant delivery or one-time packs.
  - Asking for a password.
  - Likes, views or comments sold on their own: they come inside every package, from the same
    real people.
- **Terms.** Numbers are typical monthly ranges, never promises. Customers can cancel any time,
  and the paid month runs to its end.
- **Payment.** Only by card through Stripe (Apple Pay and Google Pay where the device supports
  them), as a monthly subscription. No PayPal, crypto, UPI, Paytm, GCash, M-Pesa, MoMo, Klarna or
  bank transfer.
- **Platforms.** Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. YouTube speaks of subscribers.
- **Who we are.** A product of Sterr Agency (Sterr Technologies OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia;
  headquarters in Turkey).
- **Voice.** An honest growth agency: warm, clear, concrete. No hype, no exclamation marks.

## The idea of these pages

People search for many things we don't sell: cheap, instant, free, panels, competitor names,
payment methods, tiny or huge numbers. **Every search gets a page that answers that search
honestly and leads to the package.**

- Lead with the searcher's own words and question (title, h1, first lines).
- Answer it straight, including "no, we don't do that, and here's why".
- Show what works instead.
- Never pretend to sell what we don't.
- Each page needs its own reason to exist: different questions, angles and FAQs, not the same
  text with a word swapped. Pages on the same service must not repeat each other's sentences.

### Angles by intent

| Intent | Angle |
|---|---|
| **cheap / price** | Cost per real follower. {starter_price} a month for {starter_audience} new followers is a small amount per real person. "$1 for 1,000" buys bots that disappear and hurt reach. |
| **fast / instant** | Instant means bots: no real person follows seconds after a payment. The campaign starts quickly and real people arrive every day from the start. The speed people really want is steady, visible growth. |
| **free / free trial** | Free apps and "free followers" mean bots, follow-for-follow or giving your password away. Say clearly we don't offer free followers or a trial. Offer what is free: the calculator tool on the page (engagement or growth), plus "cancel any time". |
| **SMM panel / reseller / Discord / Telegram** | Panels resell bot traffic in bulk. Growsterr is an agency with a team and a measured dashboard. |
| **verified / celebrity / female accounts / AI / custom comments** | No one can deliver that with real people. Whoever promises it uses fake accounts or scripts. Real people write in their own words, and comments come inside packages (typically {starter_comments} a month with {starter_name}). |
| **real / active / organic / targeted / gradual / no-drop / safe** | Our home ground: explain how each of these is true for us, concretely. |
| **reels / shorts / fyp / live / comment-likes / high-retention / promote** | What real viewers do there, and how the package helps. Don't promise placement on the For You page or a live audience at a given time. |
| **private-account** | Real people can only request to follow a private account. The campaign works best on a public profile, so we ask customers to keep it public while it runs. |
| **monthly / auto** | This is exactly our model: a monthly package. |
| **viral** | No one can promise virality. Steady real growth gives each post more people who might share it. |

### Pages by type

- **Quantities ("{quantity} followers").** A goal, never a pack. The engine draws a pace chart
  from the packages.
  - Small numbers (5–500): "our packages bring far more than {quantity} a month; a pack of
    {quantity} for a few cents is bots".
  - Huge numbers (100,000, 1,000,000): "months, not minutes", and honest about it.
  - Views quantity pages must set `"plan"` (views have no typical ratio).
- **Countries.** Audience targeting is real: the customer names the country or cities.
  - Write for that country: its cities from the plan, local habits, the language the audience
    speaks. Prices are in USD; don't convert currencies.
  - Never claim an office, local team, local payment methods or local laws.
  - A country is a "country", never "our market".
- **Competitor alternatives (kind "alternative").** Name the competitor only as what the reader
  searched ("Looking for a {name} alternative?").
  - Make NO claims about the competitor at all: not what they sell, not how they deliver, not
    whether they use bots, not prices, not reviews.
  - Describe how Growsterr works and what to check in any growth service (real people, no
    password, gradual, a measured dashboard, monthly and cancellable).
  - No logos, no "vs" tables with their features.
  - If the "competitor" is a publication (Business Insider, Influencer Marketing Hub), treat
    the page as "if you read about growth services there, here's how we work".
- **Payment pages (kind "payment").** One page per method, all platforms. Answer the question:
  - For Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit card and debit card: yes, through Stripe's secure checkout.
  - For PayPal, crypto, Klarna, UPI, Paytm, GCash, M-Pesa, MoMo, mobile money and coin apps:
    "we don't take X; you can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, monthly", and why a monthly
    subscription is paid that way.
- **Guides (kind "guide").** Real answers with substance: what the platform rewards, what buying
  bots does, how to grow, what to check before paying anyone. "Do celebrities buy followers"
  must not name or accuse anyone. YouTube monetization: purchased fake engagement doesn't help
  and risks the channel; we never promise monetization or watch hours.
- **Agency pages (kind "agency").** Growsterr for brands and creators.
  - What's true: monthly growth packages for any account (creators, personal brands,
    businesses) run as promoted campaigns to real people through our partner network,
    audience targeting by place, a team behind every campaign, and a measured dashboard.
  - For anything beyond the packages (influencer campaigns with creators, custom brand
    campaigns), the page says "talk to us" (WhatsApp or email) without promising specific
    deliverables, clients, case studies or results.
  - Don't invent services we can't point to: no seeding, gifting, UGC production or ads
    management as finished offers. Answer those searches honestly: what we do, and that larger
    campaigns start with a conversation.
  - Agency country pages: we work remotely with brands and creators there and target audiences
    there. No local office.
  - "creators" and "creators/apply": the partner side, for creators who want to grow with us or
    work with us. Applying means writing to us.

## Format

Python files in `apps/pages/content/en/`, written exactly like the existing
`apps/pages/content/en/instagram.py`.

**Grid pages** (`<platform>_<part>.py` with `PAGES = [...]`):
- Variant fields from the plan row: `service`, `quantity`, `modifier`, `country` (ISO code).
- Hubs are never in these files.

**Articles** (`articles_<part>.py` with `ARTICLES = [...]`):
- `"article": <key from plan>`, `"kind": <kind>`, `"platform": <plan platform>` (sets the
  words and the order button).
- No service, quantity, modifier or country.

**Every page** has:
- `slug`, `label` (≤60), `title` (20–65 characters, ending "| Growsterr" when it fits),
  `description` (70–160), `h1` (a string or a (text, highlighted) tuple, ≤90), `intro`
  (60–420), `hero`, `faqs` (at least 3, better 5–6) and `sections` (2–4; each `h2` plus `p`
  and/or `ul`).
- Optional: `tool` (from the plan), `plan` (views quantities), `compare`, `included`, `steps`.
- Every length is measured after the placeholders are filled.

**Images:** there are no new images. Use these existing ids for `hero` and section images:
- Hubs: `instagram-hub`, `tiktok-hub`, `youtube-hub`.
- Instagram: `instagram-followers`, `instagram-followers-1000`, `instagram-creator-studio`,
  `instagram-real-people`.
- TikTok: `tiktok-likes`, `tiktok-creator`, `tiktok-watch-time`.
- YouTube: `youtube-subscribers`, `youtube-channel`, `youtube-viewers`.

Write a fresh alt text for every use.

**Slugs:**

| Page | Slug |
|---|---|
| Grid | `<platform>/<service>/<modifier>`, e.g. `instagram/followers/no-drop` |
| Quantity | `<platform>/<service>/<N>` |
| Country | `<platform>/<service>/<country-name>`: `united-states`, `united-kingdom`, `india`, `united-arab-emirates`, `south-africa` |
| Guide | `guides/<key>` |
| Alternative | `alternatives/<name>`, e.g. `alternatives/buzzoid` |
| Payment | `payments/<method>`, e.g. `payments/paypal`, `payments/apple-pay` |
| Intent article | `tiktok/shares`, `instagram/by-country` |
| Agency, main | `influencer-agency`, `tiktok-agency`, `instagram-growth-agency`, `influencer-marketing/instagram`, `creators`, `creators/apply`, `influencer-agency/pricing` |
| Agency, country | `influencer-agency/<country-name>` |

**Placeholders:** only those in docs/CONTENT.md. `{quantity}` only on quantity pages.

**Keywords:**
- Put the plan row's `primary_keyword` (the highest-volume one) naturally into the title, h1 and
  first paragraph.
- Spread the other keywords of the row across headings, text and FAQ questions, the way a
  person would phrase them, never as a list.
- Never use the word "keyword" or talk about search engines.

## Copy rules (checked by `python manage.py check_pages`)

- **Never:** SEO or search engines, "guarantee", free, crypto, celebrity or verified comments,
  gender targeting, testimonials, ratings, Trustpilot, invented statistics, invented customers.
- **The page's own word is allowed** on its own intent page, and only there:
  - "free" on `free` and `free-trial` pages, guides, tools and intent articles;
  - "verified" and "celebrity" on `verified` pages and guides;
  - "female/male" on `female` pages;
  - "crypto" on payment pages.

  Even there, say plainly that we don't sell it.
- "Instant" only in the sense "we don't; instant means bots".
- Competitor names only on their own alternative page.

## Checking (each writer, before finishing)

1. Write each file to your scratch folder first, run `python -m py_compile` on it, then copy it
   into `apps/pages/content/en/`. Never leave a half-written or broken file there: other writers
   load the same registry.
2. Run `. .venv/bin/activate && python manage.py check_pages --lang en 2>&1 | grep <your file name>`.
   Fix every error and warning in your files. Problems in other writers' files are not yours.
3. Run `python manage.py check_pages --lang en | grep "same title\|same description"` to check
   that none of yours collide.
