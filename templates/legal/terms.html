Growsterr sells monthly growth packages for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts. Each package is a promotion campaign for your public profile (your channel, on YouTube):

Your account has to stay public while your package runs, so people can see it and follow you. If it's private or unavailable, or its username changes, delivery can't continue until that's fixed. If you change your username, please tell us.

{% endsection %} {% section "subscription" %}

Every package is a monthly subscription. This is how it works:

It renews every month
Your package renews automatically every month, on the date of your first payment, and the monthly price is charged until you cancel. Each payment covers one more month of delivery.
A reminder before every renewal
We email you 3 days before each renewal with the renewal date, the amount and how to cancel.
Cancel any time
Cancel in your dashboard with the “Cancel subscription” button, or under “Manage billing or cancel”. It takes a moment, and you don't need to contact us or give a reason. We confirm your cancellation by email right away.
It ends with the period you've paid for
Canceling stops the next payment. Your package keeps running until the end of the period you've already paid for, and then it ends. Changed your mind? You can keep your subscription from your dashboard until that date.
Any growth still owed stays with us
Some months may fall below the growth target for your paid package. For this purpose, the target is the lower end of the package's monthly follower range (subscribers on YouTube), unless we confirmed a different target with you in writing. For example, if the monthly target is 1,000 new followers and we deliver 800, we record the remaining 200 as growth still owed to you. We add together any outstanding shortfalls from paid months and deduct only the additional growth delivered to make them up. While your subscription continues, this additional growth is separate from each new month's target and does not reduce it.
Canceling does not erase your outstanding balance
Canceling your subscription in Growsterr or Stripe, or removing a payment method from Stripe, does not erase growth we still owe you for paid months. We calculate the total remaining balance and continue the additional promotion needed to complete it, without requiring a new subscription, another payment or an active payment method. Your profile must remain public and available, and we need its current username to continue delivery. There is no fixed completion date because real people choose whether to follow. This balance is measured in followers or subscribers to be delivered, not money or a Stripe credit; it does not replace any refund or other remedy required by law. Removing a payment method alone does not cancel your subscription; use the cancellation options above to stop renewal.
The paid month isn't refunded
Your package starts with the payment and runs every day of the month, so the month you've paid for isn't refunded, in whole or in part, even if you cancel during it. The cases where we do refund are in {% ref "refunds" %}.
A failed payment pauses delivery
If a renewal payment doesn't go through, we email you and pause your package until it's paid. Update your payment method under “Manage billing or cancel” in your dashboard, and delivery starts again as soon as the payment goes through. The month still ends on its usual date: paused days aren't added on. If the payment still can't be collected after Stripe's retries, we may end the subscription. This pause does not remove our obligation to complete outstanding growth from paid months without another payment.
