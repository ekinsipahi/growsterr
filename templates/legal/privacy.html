{% section "who-we-are" %}

{{ company.legal_name }} runs Growsterr and is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We're a private limited company registered in Estonia under registry code {{ company.registry_code }}, at {{ company.address }}.

This policy covers our website ({{ site_host }}), your Growsterr account and our packages. For anything about your data, write to {{ contact_email }}.

{% endsection %} {% section "data" %}
  • Your account. Your email address and your password, which we store only as a one-way hash, so nobody can read it. If you continue with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us. We also note the language you use on our site.
  • Your package. The platform, your public username and profile link, the location you'd like most of your audience to come from, the package and its price, and when it started, renews or ends.
  • Your phone number. You give it on the order form so we can stay in touch with you about your package, by phone or message.
  • Payments. Stripe handles them. We keep the amount, date and status of your payments, and Stripe's reference numbers for you, your subscription and your payments. Your card details never reach us.
  • Delivery numbers. For each day of your package, Linksterr, our delivery network, measures how many people your profile was shown to, the visits it got, the new followers counted and the countries the visits came from. We also keep your account's public follower count and an estimate for the next 30 days. Your dashboard shows the visits, countries and new followers; we keep all of these numbers as delivery records.
  • Your acceptance of the terms. When you tick the box on the order form: the date and time, the version of the terms, your IP address and your browser.
  • Account activity. Sign-ins, visits to your dashboard (at most one record an hour) and visits to the cancel page, each with the time, IP address and browser. We keep these as evidence in case a payment is disputed.
  • How you found us. The page you ordered from, and any campaign tags or ad click ID in the link that brought you, such as utm_source or gclid.
  • Messages. What you write to us, and the emails we send you.
  • Server logs. Like any website, our servers log requests: IP address, page, time and browser.

We never ask for the password of your Instagram, TikTok or YouTube account, and we don't need any access to it.

{% endsection %} {% section "retention" %}
  • Your account, packages, delivery numbers and activity records: for as long as your account is open.
  • When you ask us to delete your account: we delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except what we must keep. That's the payment and invoice records accounting law requires us to keep (in Estonia, generally 7 years), and the records about a subscription that we need while a payment can still be disputed (up to 18 months after your last payment) or while a dispute is open.
  • Orders you started but didn't pay for: they stay in your account so you can finish them, until you ask us to delete them or your account.
  • Server logs: kept for a short time by our host, then deleted.
{% endsection %} {% section "security" %}

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere, store passwords only as one-way hashes, give access only to the people who need it, and choose providers that protect data to high standards. If a breach ever puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

{% endsection %} {% section "rights" %}

Under the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, with the exceptions in {% ref "retention" %};
  • restrict how we use it;
  • hand over the data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests, if you object;
  • stop using it where you gave consent, if you withdraw it.

Write to us at {{ contact_email }}, ideally from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm it's you. We answer within one month, and we don't charge for it.

We don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.

You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee) or to the data protection authority where you live or work.
